How does the book recommendation quiz work?

Our free book quiz asks you five simple questions about your mood, how much time you have, which part of the world interests you, what you value most in a book, and how much of a challenge you're looking for. Based on your answers, our scoring system matches you with the book from our curated list of 18 essential works that fits you best right now.

What kinds of books does the quiz recommend?

The quiz draws from a handpicked selection of the world's most essential literary fiction — including works by Camus, García Márquez, Dostoevsky, Toni Morrison, Orwell, Kafka, Tolstoy, Murakami, Virginia Woolf, Chinua Achebe, Margaret Atwood, Ishiguro, Han Kang, Borges, Kawabata, Mahfouz, Tokarczuk, Arundhati Roy, Adiga, Tayeb Salih, Ngũgĩ wa Thiong'o, Bulgakov, Tóibín, Viet Thanh Nguyen, George Eliot, and Jeffrey Eugenides. These are books that have stood the test of time, won Nobel and Booker Prizes, and changed the course of world literature.

I don't know what to read next — can this quiz help?
