How does the book recommendation quiz work?

Our free book quiz asks you three simple questions — how you're feeling right now, how much time you have, and how much of a reading challenge you're looking for. Based on your answers, our system matches you with the book from our curated list of 28 essential works that fits you best right now.

What kinds of books does the quiz recommend?

The quiz draws from a handpicked selection of the world's most essential literary fiction — including works by Camus, García Márquez, Dostoevsky, Toni Morrison, Orwell, Kafka, Tolstoy, Murakami, Virginia Woolf, Chinua Achebe, Margaret Atwood, Ishiguro, Han Kang, Borges, Kawabata, Mahfouz, Tokarczuk, Arundhati Roy, Adiga, Tayeb Salih, Ngũgĩ wa Thiong'o, Bulgakov, Tóibín, Viet Thanh Nguyen, George Eliot, Jeffrey Eugenides, Muriel Barbery, Carlos Ruiz Zafón, and Yukio Mishima.

I don't know what to read next — can this quiz help?

Absolutely. Whether you're an experienced reader looking for your next great book, or someone who hasn't read literary fiction before and doesn't know where to start, the quiz is designed to give you a confident, personalised recommendation in under 30 seconds.
